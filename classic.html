Research

01

Efficient AI in the physical world

Physical AI and on-device machine learning: compact models that perceive and run on resource-constrained hardware.

SpatialNet: Power-efficient, Effortless Onboarding with Smart Glass

Under review CSE-450 Capstone · Dec 2025–Sep 2026

Developed at BUET with Samsung R&D Institute Bangladesh. A 0.72M-parameter, 1.5 MB model finds the wearer’s hand, fingertip, and pointed-at target from a single frame, fully on a Samsung WearOS-based smart glass at 60–80 ms/frame.

82.6% target IoU and 99.2% scene accuracy; 34 percentage points higher IoU than vision-language models with 5,000× fewer parameters.

Md. Mosharaf Hossain Apurbo, Niloy Das Robin, Nabiha Tahseen, Gourove Roy, Sayjad Rahman

03

Graph algorithms & generative structure

Generating Scale-Free Chordal Graphs — ongoing undergraduate thesis at BUET, Jun 2026–Present, supervised by Dr. Md. Saidur Rahman, Professor, CSE.

Characterised the degree distributions achievable by chordal graphs of bounded clique number, with the same condition for k-degenerate graphs and graphs of bounded treewidth and pathwidth.

Designed a linear-time generator for a prescribed degree distribution, producing a perfect elimination order as a certificate. For power laws, it achieves the optimal convergence rate without a degree cut-off.
