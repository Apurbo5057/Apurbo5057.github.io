\section{Research Experience}
\entry{Prescribed Degree Distributions in Chordal Graphs of Bounded Clique Number}{Ongoing}
      {Undergraduate Thesis, BUET --- Supervisor: \blink{https://saidurrahman.buet.ac.bd/}{Dr.\ Md.\ Saidur Rahman} (Professor, Dept.\ of CSE)}{Jun 2026 -- Present}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Exactly characterised which degree distributions are achievable by chordal graphs of bounded clique number, and showed the same condition holds for $k$-degenerate, bounded-treewidth and bounded-pathwidth graphs.
  \item Designed a \textbf{linear-time generator} that outputs chordal graphs with a prescribed degree distribution (with a perfect elimination order as certificate); for power laws it attains the \textbf{optimal convergence rate} with no degree cut-off.
\end{itemize}
